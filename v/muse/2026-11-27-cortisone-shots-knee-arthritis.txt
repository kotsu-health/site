Cortisone shots for knee arthritis: a Cochrane review of 27 trials (1,767 people) found pain relief that was moderate at 1–2 weeks, small at 13 weeks and not detectable at 26 weeks; the reviewers rated the evidence low quality. In a two-year trial of 140 people, a shot every 3 months gave no better pain than saline and slightly more cartilage thinning (0.21 vs 0.10 mm); another trial found no extra joint-space loss. A hot, swollen or locking knee needs assessment.
