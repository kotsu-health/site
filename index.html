Our promise

Every claim carries a grade.

Each article opens with a table that rates its main claims from A to D, with the studies behind them one click away. When the research is weak, we say so — especially when the topic is fashionable.

Read the full method →

  • A · StrongSystematic reviews or randomized trials point the same way
  • B · ModerateConsistent observational studies; cause and effect not proven
  • C · EarlySmall, short, animal or lab studies only
  • D · UnsupportedNo good evidence, or the evidence points against it
