If you are considering treatment in Japan

Japan regulates regenerative treatments, including platelet-rich plasma (PRP) and related injections, under a dedicated national law; clinics must file a treatment plan before offering them. Some of these treatments involve a blood draw and a second visit, so they need planning around the length of your stay.

The author's practice page explains the options, what the evidence shows, costs and risks.

Before you decide

  • Read the evidence first. Our articles grade each claim from A to D, including for treatments that are popular or expensive.
  • Bring your imaging and reports. Recent X-rays or MRI scans, on disc or as files, make a first visit far more useful.
  • Ask about follow-up. Some treatments need more than one visit or rehabilitation afterwards.
